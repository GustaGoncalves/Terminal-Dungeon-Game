# What The Game Is About?
It's a very simple and straight foward turn-based, terminal-based, roguelike, very original I know.
The whole Idea is that, there's this labyrinth that people just go in for a bunch of weird mysterious reasons
and you take charge of being one, of three characters. Unfortunately, only one exists in this version.

The whole gimmick would be the neat little skill system I made, which would be the main way for you to improve your odds of finishing the game.
There are other things too, but they're here to just offer flexibility in how you approach the game.

There's a couple of little lore tidbits (Yes, I got THIS invested in this dumb game, don't judge) sprinkled in item and skill descriptions and some of the currently available dialogue.
Each character has their own unique motivation to be in the labyrinth, which are hinted mostly by their dialogue responses, classes and overall personalities.
